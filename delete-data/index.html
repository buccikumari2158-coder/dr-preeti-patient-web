You can ask for the data you entered in the Dr. Preeti Samal Android app or on drpreetisamal.online to be deleted at any time. Both are run by Dr. Preeti Samal’s clinic in Jajpur, Odisha, India.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 95837 89411, or call the clinic on +91 95837 89411.
  2. Say that you want your app data deleted, and give the name and mobile number you used in the app, plus your email if you entered one.
  3. The clinic checks that the request is yours, deletes the data, and replies to confirm, within 30 days.

There is no login in the app, so the clinic finds your records by your name and mobile number.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email, age, gender, address, blood group and emergency contact.
  • Your appointment requests and booking history.
  • Messages and notifications the clinic sent to you in the app.
  • Your push notification ID.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Visit and prescription records that the law requires the clinic to keep as medical records are kept for that period, normally 3 years from your last visit, and then deleted.
  • Advertising data is held by Google AdMob, not the clinic. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads.

Uninstalling is not the same as deleting

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the guest session from your phone, but it does not delete the records stored by the clinic. To delete those, follow the steps above.
