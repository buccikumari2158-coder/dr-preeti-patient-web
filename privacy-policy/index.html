This policy explains what information the Dr. Preeti Samal Android app and the website drpreetisamal.online (together, “the app”) collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. The app is run by Dr. Preeti Samal’s clinic in Jajpur, Odisha, India (“the clinic”, “we”, “us”).

In short

  • We collect only what is needed to book and manage your appointments.
  • Your health details are seen only by you and the clinic, and are never used for advertising.
  • The Android app shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. The website shows no ads.
  • We never sell your information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, which the app asks for the first time you open it.
  • When you book an appointment: phone number, email address, age, gender, the reason for your visit, any notes, and the date and time you choose.
  • If you fill in Settings: your address, blood group and an emergency contact. These are optional.

Information the clinic adds

  • The status of your appointments, prescriptions the doctor issues to you, and messages the clinic sends you.

Information collected automatically

  • An anonymous app ID. There is no login or password. The app creates a random ID (using Firebase Authentication) that links your records to the app on your device.
  • A push notification ID (OneSignal), so the clinic can send you appointment updates. Android app only.
  • Your device’s advertising ID and basic technical information (device model, operating system version, IP address and app version), used by Google AdMob to show ads. Android app only.

What we do not collect

  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.

2. How we use your information

  • To book, confirm, reschedule or cancel your appointments and show you your history.
  • To let the doctor and clinic staff prepare for your visit and give you prescriptions.
  • To send you notifications about your appointments and the clinic.
  • To show ads that keep the app free (see section 4).
  • To keep the app secure and to fix problems.

3. Your health information

The reason for your visit, your notes and your prescriptions are health information. We use them only to provide your care. They are visible only to you, in the app, and to the clinic. They are never shared with advertisers or used to choose ads.

4. Advertising

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and information about how the app is used.

  • All ads in the app are non-personalised: we ask Google not to choose them from your profile or interests.
  • We never pass your appointment details, reason for visit or any other health information to Google.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with:

  • Google Firebase, which stores your records and runs the anonymous sign-in (privacy information).
  • OneSignal, which delivers push notifications (privacy policy).
  • Google AdMob, which shows ads as described above (privacy policy).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of patients or the clinic.

These providers handle data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies.

6. How we protect your information

Your records are stored on Google’s Firebase servers and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Security rules enforced by Firebase let each patient see only their own records; only the clinic’s admin account can see all patients. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Guest sessions and changing phones

Because there is no login, your records are tied to the app on your device. If you uninstall the app, clear its data or change phones, the app starts a new guest session and you will no longer see your earlier history in the app. The clinic still keeps your records, so contact us if you need them.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as it is needed to provide your care, and for as long as the law requires medical records to be kept. After that, it is deleted.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and update your details in the app’s Settings.
  • Cancel an appointment in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it.

Deleting your data: contact us (section 12) with the name and phone number you used in the app. We will delete your profile, appointment history, notifications and push ID within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

10. Children

The app is not meant to be used by children on their own. A parent or guardian should book appointments for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
